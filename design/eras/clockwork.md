# Age

A settlement of the Age of Letters that has proven the [Courier Depot](/blueprints/depot.md) in use, with one standing, has entered the first age of machines ([ages](/systems/ages.md)). Its own blueprints, which only a settlement of this age can think of, are the [Windmill](/blueprints/windmill.md), gears that grind the grain for the bakers, and the [Conveyor](/blueprints/conveyor.md), the machine tier after bots: a belt that carries goods between the buildings beside it as far as it runs. Rail, the tier after belts, comes later.
