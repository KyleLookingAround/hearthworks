# Age

A settlement of the Age of Letters that knows the [Courier Depot](/blueprints/depot.md) has entered the first age of machines. Its own blueprint, which only a settlement of this age can think of, is the [Windmill](/blueprints/windmill.md): gears that grind the grain for the bakers. Conveyors and rail, the machine tiers after bots, come later.
