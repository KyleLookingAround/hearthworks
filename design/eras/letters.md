# Age

A settlement of the Age of Wheel and Keel that has proven in use both the [Library](/blueprints/library.md) and the [University](/blueprints/university.md) (`share` 1), with `works` of them standing, has entered this age: one that keeps knowledge and pursues it. Since only a village or a town plans a university, a hamlet does not reach it ([ages](/systems/ages.md)).
