* **Finding**: Tuning knowledge took four corrections, each in the game rather than the gate. (1) "Carriers busy" is no strain signal: carriers find surplus runs to storage, so it reads 0.96 to 0.99 with or without depots. The share on requested deliveries reads 0.65 to 0.9 without depots and about 0.5 with them, so that is the hauling measure. (2) Hauling never outranked "no free beds" (severity 1), so villages that knew the depot never built it and forgot it; growth now carries `growth_weight` 0.7 and hauling `haul_weight` 3. (3) A depot that reached nothing new was still placed, repeatedly (14 depots in a village of 18); a depot spot must now reach buildings no bots reach. (4) With only proven knowledge travelling, both villages came up with the depot independently on two seeds before it could spread; ideas now travel unproven, carrying their verifications, and visitors no longer wait for a carrier who is completely idle.
