# Design bundle log

## 2026-10-03

* **Update**: [Roadmap](/roadmap.md) after Kyle's direction. A new game starts with two settlements on any map size (one to four on the new-game screen). Trade between neighbours moves up to Phase 13, on foot with barter, so two villages can specialise long before carts. People becomes People and traditions (14): apprenticeships, and the dead honoured by each village's own custom (burial, cremation, ship burial), which spread and change like knowledge. A new Learning phase (15) adds libraries (knowledge kept from being forgotten), schools and universities. Later phases move up by one, to 20.
* **Update**: [Roadmap](/roadmap.md) Phase 7 becomes Worlds, after Kyle's direction. The player picks a map type (Island, Landmass and Coast as OKF concepts in a new `design/maps/` folder; Archipelago with ships in Phase 17), a size (small to huge), a seed and starting settlements. One standard map (Island, medium) is the default game and the world every gate runs on; a map suite checks every type and size on three seeds. Every gate moves to the standard map in Phase 7 under decision 0004. Until then the standard is today's small island.
* **Update**: [Roadmap](/roadmap.md) restructured into 19 phases in five arcs, with a "needs" column, cross-cutting tracks (performance budgets, versioned saves, gate upkeep, content), and for each phase: why now, what it builds, a proposed gate with metric names, which existing gates it disturbs, and open questions for Kyle. Changes from the previous draft: the old Phase 6 splits into Solid ground (6) and Big islands (7); districts move wholly into Village to town (9); the player's levers come right after it (10) instead of last; seasons follow the deeper economy (12) so food is balanced once; hazards close the list (19) with the hard laws. Proposes splitting `fed_min` (hunger) from `mood_min` once surroundings count, so older gates keep their intent.
* **Creation**: [Decision 0004](/decisions/0004-reworking-gates.md): gates may be reworked as the game grows (Kyle's call). Never to turn a failing gate green; revise in place with a `# Revisions` entry when the intent is kept, otherwise supersede and deprecate the old gate. Loosening is always reported. `npm run gates` and `npm test` now skip deprecated gates, which can still be run by name.
* **Update**: [Roadmap](/roadmap.md) Phase 8 adds, after Kyle's direction, a ladder of home sizes (a cottage torn down for a terrace when the town outgrows it) and a planning hierarchy. A town planner sets districts, streets and quotas; district planners run their own blocks and send up what they cannot solve; districts split as they grow. The planners become villagers with a planning skill in Phase 10, and the player steers the town planner in Phase 17.
* **Update**: [Roadmap](/roadmap.md) revised after Kyle's direction: buildings become solid, with doors, in Phase 6; surroundings (greenery, water, nuisance from workplaces, crowding) shape mood in Phase 7; a new Phase 8, Village to town, has settlements change form with size (roomy hamlets, paired houses, terraced rows on a street grid in towns) and lets the planner demolish and rebuild denser, under rules that re-house residents first. Later phases move up by one, to 17 in total.
* **Finding**: Villagers currently walk through buildings: path cost looks only at land, roads and trees, and a building's door tile is inside its own footprint. Recorded as Phase 6 work.
* **Update**: [Roadmap](/roadmap.md) extended to Phase 16, from big islands and saves (6) through terrain and desire-path roads (7), a deeper economy (8), individual villagers (9), transport tiers (10), daughter settlements (11), trade (12), the sea and colonies (13), ages of technology (14), seasons and hazards (15) to player levers and a chronicle (16). Each phase names a proposed gate. Adds principles that apply to every phase, including work budgets measured as deterministic counters instead of wall-clock time.
* **Creation**: [Knowledge](/systems/knowledge.md) (`src/sim/knowledge.ts`), stable. Each settlement keeps an OKF-style record per blueprint (`by`/`at`, `verified`, `from`, `used`). Blueprints with a `discovery` block are thought of under strain (the [Courier Depot](/blueprints/depot.md): `need: hauling`), proven by running well for `verify_seconds`, learned by hand when the player builds one, carried by visitors between settlements, and forgotten after `forget_after_seconds` unbuilt. Invention draws from its own random stream, so gates 1 to 3 give identical metrics on seeds 1847, 7, 42, 99, 2026 and 31337.
* **Creation**: Several settlements. UI: a Knowledge panel, one plans line per settlement, settlement names on the map, visitors in purple.

Seed 42: 2.6 / 3.1 / 5.0 / 6.2. Seed 99: 14.1 / 14.3 / 15.1 / 15.2. Seed 2026: 5.6 / 6.2 / 7.4 / 8.1. Seed 31337: 12.9 / 13.8 / 13.9 / 14.7. Every run: 1 invention, 0 departures, mood_min 0.963 or better, peak 78 to 99, 3 to 8 depots on the island.
* **Finding**: Gate 4 re-swept now that its village must invent the depot: seed 1847 peak 84, 0 departures, mood_min 0.975; seed 7: 101, 0, 0.979; seed 42: 89, 0, 0.991; seed 99: 97, 0, 0.986; seed 2026: 90, 0, 0.979; seed 31337: 92, 0, 0.979. All pass.
* **Finding**: Tuning knowledge took four corrections, each in the game rather than the gate. (1) "Carriers busy" is no strain signal: carriers find surplus runs to storage, so it reads 0.96 to 0.99 with or without depots. The share on requested deliveries reads 0.65 to 0.9 without depots and about 0.5 with them, so that is the hauling measure. (2) Hauling never outranked "no free beds" (severity 1), so villages that knew the depot never built it and forgot it; growth now carries `growth_weight` 0.7 and hauling `haul_weight` 3. (3) A depot that reached nothing new was still placed, repeatedly (14 depots in a village of 18); a depot spot must now reach buildings no bots reach. (4) With only proven knowledge travelling, both villages came up with the depot independently on two seeds before it could spread; ideas now travel unproven, carrying their verifications, and visitors no longer wait for a carrier who is completely idle.

* **Creation**: [Village planner](/systems/planner.md) (`src/sim/planner.ts`), now stable. It senses shortages of each good, beds and hands, scores blueprints by relief minus cost, follows recipe inputs (Farm before Bakery, Forester before Sawmill), places with a one-tile gap and scored links between makers and users, and keeps one site open at a time. Tuning is in its frontmatter. Off by default in the sim so gates 1 to 3 stay scripted; on by default in the game behind a **Village plans** toggle, with a status line and a "built by the village" reason in the inspector.
* **Creation**: [Gate 4](/gates/04-village-plans.md): the town grows with no build calls. Thresholds are Gate 2's (`peak_villagers >= 20`, `villagers >= 18`, `departures <= 2`, `mood_min >= 0.6` after a five-minute warm-up). Passes on seed 1847: peak 84, 0 departures, mood_min 0.979.
* **Finding**: Gate 4 seed sweep, all pass. Seed 7: peak 102, 0 departures, mood_min 0.979. Seed 42: peak 93, 0, 0.979. Seed 99: peak 99, 0, 0.988. Seed 2026: peak 90, 0, 0.979. Seed 31337: peak 92, 0, 0.975. Every run ends with 0 unfinished sites and 57 to 65 planned buildings. Towns reach 20 villagers at 6 to 7.5 minutes. The thresholds are now loose: Gate 2's script was capped at 8 houses, while the planner grows until the land runs out.
* **Update**: [Production](/systems/production.md) gains site priority, fixing the known issue found by Gate 3. Sites queue by priority, then age, and each is promised supply in turn, so an expensive site can no longer soak up the planks a more urgent one needs. New tuning `site_priority_tiles: 2` makes carriers favour urgent sites. Gates 1 to 3 give identical metrics on seeds 1847, 7, 42, 99, 2026 and 31337 (gate 3 still misses on seed 99, 0.247, as before).
* **Finding**: Three planner failures found while tuning, each fixed in the planner rather than the gate. (1) An unstaffed Sawmill read as zero planks, so the planner kept ordering wood producers it could not staff; workers are now a resource (`carrier_share`). (2) "No free beds" outranked a moderate bread shortage, so houses outran bakeries and 4 to 7 villagers left per run; growth now waits until bread is covered. (3) On seed 31337 a House site starved because three carriers always found shorter dump runs; fixed by the carrier preference above.
