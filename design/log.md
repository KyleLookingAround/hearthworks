* **Creation**: second pass, Phase 14: naming customs ([people](/systems/people.md), [settling](/systems/settling.md), [saves](/systems/saves.md)). With people on, every villager has a given name from their settlement's naming custom, which it takes from its land by the lines of its custom for the dead: by much water, names for the sea (`names_sea`); well wooded, for the trees (`names_trees`); otherwise for the fields and their birds (`names_fields`), sixteen of each. A daughter keeps its mother's custom and its settlers their names. A name is picked by the villager's id and the world's seed, never from a random stream, so a game with names runs exactly as one without (checked over 20 minutes of a default new game). Names show in a home's household and in the news of births and deaths; the first child of each settlement goes into the chronicle by name, and the Steward panel says where a settlement's names come from. Unlike feasts, the custom neither spreads nor changes, so it keeps neighbouring villages apart. Saves go to version 25; a version 24 save takes its customs from the land and names its people as it loads.
