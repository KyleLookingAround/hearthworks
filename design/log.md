* **Creation**: Phase 13, neighbours [trade](/systems/trade.md). Each settlement weighs its goods by cover (how long its stock lasts at its planner's rate of use): what runs under `want_cover` 30 seconds is wanted, what lasts beyond `keep` and `spare_cover` 60 seconds can be spared, the food chain keeping twice that and a meal a villager. Every 15 seconds a settlement sends a porter (one per 25 villagers) with up to 4 of a spare good to the neighbour that best matches, and brings back a wanted good at a rate set by both sides' want, 0.5 to 2. Imports count as supply in the [planner](/systems/planner.md); a neighbour's want of a good it makes none of counts as demand. Porters gossip as visitors. On in new games (a new-game checkbox), off in older scenarios. Trade lines in the Steward panel; saves go to version 9. [Gate 13](/gates/13-trade.md) closes the phase.
