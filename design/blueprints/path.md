# Role

What a settlement's feet wear in: the most walked tiles are paved by its [planner](/systems/planner.md) (desire paths), a town lays its streets as paths, and the player can lay them by hand, free and at once. Speed multiplier is `path_speed` in [logistics](/systems/logistics.md). Until Phase 21 these were called roads; roads proper are the [Road](/blueprints/road.md), planned straight.
