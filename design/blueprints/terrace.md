# Role

Built of [bricks](/goods/bricks.md) as well as planks. The third rung of the ladder of homes: 1.5 beds a tile, twice the [Cottage](/blueprints/house.md). A settlement plans them once it is a town (`form: town`, [planner](/systems/planner.md)), in rows along the streets it lays, and tears down old cottages to make room for them. Later rungs (tenements) come with Phase 12's seasons and Phase 20's fire, when density has a price beyond crowding.
