# Role

With a teacher at work, the settlement's children are schooled, and once grown they learn every trade `school_factor` times as fast ([people](/systems/people.md)). They read, too: with a [Library](/blueprints/library.md) in the settlement, its readers take in what other libraries hold without a visitor, and learn a trade written down there as from a master ([knowledge](/systems/knowledge.md)). The planner builds one once a settlement has `school_children` children.
